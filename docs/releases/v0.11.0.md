# 0.11.0 — Timetable Live Activities

- Enable a timetable Live Activity separately for each child in Configure.
- Select one or more Home Assistant Companion phones and Dutch or English card text.
- A new per-child switch arms the daily activity and restores its state after restart.
- Fixed native layout: current lesson with room/end time, next subject/start time,
  and the next classroom on the right and in the compact Dynamic Island.
- Starts 15 minutes before school, emphasizes the next lesson during the final
  five minutes, shows the destination during breaks and ends ten minutes after school.
- Clock transitions do not wait for Somtoday polling. Schedule changes are
  applied at the next successful source refresh. Cancelled lessons and breaks are skipped.
- Changes only generate a push when content changes. Failed destinations retry
  independently. Source failures replace stale destinations with an unavailable status.
- Existing calendars, export options and other children's settings are preserved.

After updating in HACS, restart Home Assistant. Configure the feature for the child,
choose their phone, then turn on **Name · Live Activity**. This feature requires
a compatible Home Assistant Companion app, synchronized Live Activity tokens and
access to Home Assistant from school. Actual lock-screen rendering and delivery
still need verification on a phone.
