- Compact current lesson: `Nu: Wiskunde · 218 · tot 10:10`.
- Compact upcoming lesson: `Hierna: Engels · 10:25`.
- Known full subject names are shortened for the Live Activity only, including
  Latijn, Nederlands, Engels, Duits, Frans, Grieks, Gym and Digitaal.
- Unknown subject names remain unchanged. Calendar titles and source data retain
  their original subject names.

  are explicitly identified. The left icon remains the only decorative icon.

Install through HACS beta versions and restart Home Assistant. Layout/title identity
is unchanged from beta.4, so this update does not force another card restart.
Actual truncation still depends on the phone's font size and native card layout.
