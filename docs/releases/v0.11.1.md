# 0.11.1 — Timetable Live Activities and separate appointment exports

## Changes since 0.10

- Per-child timetable Live Activity on iPhone, or Live Update on Android 16+ with
  a compatible Home Assistant Companion app. Earlier Android versions receive an
  ongoing timetable notification.
- Current subject, room and end time; upcoming subject and start time; the next
  classroom on the right under **Next room / Volgend lokaal**. Compact text and
  known shortened subject names, with changing icons only on the left.
- Starts 15 minutes before school, shows the next destination during breaks and
  ends ten minutes after the school day. Per-child switch, multiple phones and
  Dutch/English labels. Clock transitions do not depend on the API polling interval.
- Transparent iOS Lock Screen background with adaptive text colour. Tap destination
  targets the Somtoday app on iOS and Android. Final app launching and appearance
  remain dependent on the installed phone apps.
- All relevant entities grouped under one device per child; account diagnostics
  have a separate device. Existing entity IDs, dashboard references and settings
  are preserved.
- Improved refresh-token persistence and a coordinated retry after early HTTP 401
  responses reduce unnecessary reauthentication.
- The HA timetable calendar and regular timetable export now contain only source
  category **Rooster**. Two independent, optional per-child exports cover **Toets**
  appointments and all other categories. Study-guide tests remain separate.
- The options explain schools' differing use of appointment categories: a timetable
  test may be a reserved test period, and individual appointments may represent exams.
  Each export can use its own calendar and title prefix.
- HTML descriptions become readable plain text, with lists, numbers and decoded
  characters. Existing managed exported notes update without duplicate appointments.
- Startup readiness also waits for the two new destination calendars. Temporary
  beta investigation attributes are removed from the stable integration.

## After updating

Restart Home Assistant. To enable Live Activities, configure the child, select
their Companion phone(s), then enable the child's Live Activity switch. The
Companion app must support the feature and reach Home Assistant from school.

Existing timetable exports are now limited to **Rooster**. Enable the new optional
exports if you want Toets or other categories included; choose their destinations
per child. Both new exports default to off. Existing study-guide test export remains
available independently. No account removal or re-add is needed.
