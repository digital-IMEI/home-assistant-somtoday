# 0.11.1-beta.3 — Readable notes and timetable Live Activity styling

- Converts Somtoday HTML descriptions to readable calendar notes, retaining
  paragraphs, bullets, numbered lists and decoded characters. Applies to native
  calendars, exported appointment descriptions and homework.
- Existing managed agenda items are updated through the normal reconciliation,
  including removal of obsolete category exports without creating duplicates.
- Taps on timetable Live Activities target the Somtoday app: `somtoday://` on iOS
  and `app://nl.topicus.somtoday.leerling` on Android.
- Requests a transparent iOS Lock Screen card with adaptive text colour. Android
  keeps its native notification background.
- Adds subject emoji labels and a changing main icon for the current or upcoming
  lesson, breaks, the start of school and the end of the school day.
- Live Activities use classroom appointments in Rooster and Toets categories;
  individual appointments cannot take over the displayed lesson or destination.

All appointment-category exports from beta.2 remain available. Authentication
recovery, per-child device grouping, export migration and platform-specific payloads
were reviewed and tested together. App launch and final appearance require a phone
check after installation.

Install through HACS beta versions and restart Home Assistant. Existing managed
calendar notes update on the next successful calendar synchronization.
