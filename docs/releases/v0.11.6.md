# v0.11.6 — consecutive lessons of the same subject

- iPhone Live Activities and Android Live Updates show adjoining lessons of the same subject in the same known classroom as one continuous block, with the final end time.
- The next lesson and next classroom refer to the lesson after that block, and the final-five-minute phase uses the block's end time.
- If a break or classroom change separates repeated subjects, the next line says `Hierna: muziek verder` / `Next: music continues`. Breaks keep the actual restart time, including while the break is in progress.
- Classroom changes, unknown classrooms and different appointment categories remain separate; calendars and exports retain the original appointments.
