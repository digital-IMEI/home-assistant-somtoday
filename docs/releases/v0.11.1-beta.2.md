# 0.11.1-beta.2 — Separate appointment-category exports

- The native HA timetable calendar and regular timetable export now contain only
  appointments in the Somtoday **Rooster** category.
- Per child, optionally export **Toets** timetable appointments to their own calendar.
  The options explain that some schools use this instead of the study guide and
  may also schedule test periods without an actual test.
- Optionally export **non-regular lessons and appointments**: all remaining categories,
  including individual appointments and activities. The options explain schools'
  varying use of these categories.
- Each new export has a separate destination and editable prefix. Original times,
  locations and descriptions are preserved. Destinations may be shared.
- Study-guide tests remain an independent export. Both new exports default to off;
  source categories determine routing without title or colour matching.

After updating and restarting Home Assistant, configure the child and select the
exports and destination calendars. To include previously exported non-Rooster
appointments, enable the appropriate new exports. For the green individual
proefwerk example, enable non-regular lessons and appointments.

School-day calculations continue to include active non-break school appointments.
This beta retains appointment metadata for investigation and the previously
prepared Android Live Update, authentication and per-child device changes.
