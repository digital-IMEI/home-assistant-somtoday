# v0.11.9 — combine consecutive test periods

- Consecutive Somtoday test slots for the same subject are combined into one exported calendar event. For example, music from 10:25–11:15 and 11:15–12:05 appears as a single test from 10:25–12:05.
- Preserves all classroom locations and nonduplicate descriptions across the combined slots.
- The built-in assessments calendar and assessment exports also combine adjacent timed study-guide tests when subject, assessment type, and topic match.
- Different subjects, topics, separate time blocks, and all-day assignments remain independent.

Update via HACS and restart Home Assistant to use the new version.
