# 0.11.2 — Silent timetable activity updates

- Sets the Live Activity `silent` flag on every start, update and source-outage
  payload. ActivityKit alerts are independent of ordinary notification sound,
  so `push.sound: null` alone did not suppress their sound and haptics.
- iOS payloads also use passive interruption level and no notification sound to
  quiet standard-notification fallbacks.
- Android retains `alert_once`, the low-importance timetable channel and requests
  a zero-duration vibration pattern. Existing activity identity is unchanged.
- The activity continues to display current/upcoming classroom information and
  update in place; unchanged content is still not resent.

Update in HACS and restart Home Assistant. No reconfiguration or new activity tag
is required. iOS may batch silent pushes, and Android channel settings configured
on the phone can override channel defaults.
