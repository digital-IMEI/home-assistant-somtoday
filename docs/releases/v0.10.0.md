## New: absence overview and lesson registrations

**A big thank you to [AlferNL](https://github.com/AlferNL) for creating this feature, testing it against the Somtoday portal and carefully addressing the review feedback in [PR #3](https://github.com/digital-IMEI/home-assistant-somtoday/pull/3)!**

Enable **Absence overview** per child in the Somtoday integration settings to add two sensors:

- **Absences:** school-year counts for authorised/unauthorised absences, late arrivals and removals, with recent registration details.
- **Lesson registrations:** homework not completed and materials not in order, including subject, lesson hour and classroom. Outstanding measures are shown when available.

Use the sensors in dashboards and your own automations. This feature is read-only and **disabled by default**.

### Privacy and reliability

- School wording requires a separate opt-in. If enabled, this text may also be retained in Home Assistant history and backups.
- Unreadable or invalid data is reported as unavailable, never as zero registrations. Other sources continue updating.
- Includes Dutch/English entity names, consistent time zones and privacy-safe source diagnostics.

### Updating

Restart Home Assistant after updating. Existing settings are retained. To use the new feature, open **Settings → Devices & services → Somtoday → Configure**, select your child and enable **Absence overview**.

Validation: 158 tests and 2 subtests passed on Python 3.14 with Home Assistant 2026.8.2.
